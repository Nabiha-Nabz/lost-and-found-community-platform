Why this platform exists

Informal lost-property posts are scattered, hard to search, and often expose more personal information than necessary. Lost & Found gives communities a structured alternative: reports use consistent details, public pages show only safe information, and ownership claims are reviewed privately.

Privacy before publicity

Every report includes private identifying details that are kept away from public pages. Claimants provide their own private answer, and an administrator compares the information before approving a claim.

Post an item

Report lost or found property

The reporter submits public details, photos, a general location, map coordinates, and a private feature. The report remains pending until reviewed.

Administrator

Review and publish safely

An administrator checks each report before it appears in browsing and map results. Inappropriate or incomplete reports can be rejected.

Claim Item

Search and submit a claim

A seeker browses approved items and submits a private ownership answer. Duplicate claims are prevented.

Administrator

Verify and resolve

The administrator compares both private accounts, records a decision, notifies the users, and resolves the item when a claim is approved.
